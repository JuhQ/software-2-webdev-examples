### Web Development 5 cr

**Assessment:** HTML+CSS assignment, 2 web development exams

- **HTML+CSS assignment:** assessed as pass/fail. An incomplete or late submission lowers the final exam grade by one (-1). A detailed description of the assessment is in the assignment.
- Two partial exams and two resit opportunities. One exam grade is given based on the combined points of the partial exams, and this exam grade determines the grade for the whole component.
- You must get at least 10 points per module from the programming assignments.

## Attendance Tracking

Teachers record attendance with the JacSec application

- You can see your attendance percentage for each subject in the application.
